COLOUR IN FIVE CHECKS

We never go from the chair to the bowl in one move. A new shade passes five checks, and you watch each one being ticked.

  1. Consult

    Twenty free minutes at the window mirror, in daylight: your base, your history and how much upkeep you really want.

  2. Strand test

    A hidden strand takes the mix first, so the shade on your head is the shade we promised, not a surprise.

  3. Lighten

    Only where the sun would naturally catch, and only as far as the strand test said the hair can go.

  4. Tone

    The raw lift is tuned warm, cool or ash, to your skin and to the hard Marseille light.

  5. Gloss

    A sealing gloss, a cold rinse and a written aftercare card. Your next visit is already pencilled in.

MISTRAL

BUILT FOR A HUNDRED WINDY DAYS

The mistral comes down the Rhône valley and through Marseille on around a hundred days a year, cold, dry and stubborn. A cut that only works in a still room is not finished here. We cut from the inside: weight where the wind pulls, texture where it lifts, a line that drops back once the gust has passed.

“I cycle down the Canebière every morning. This is the first haircut that arrives at work looking like it left home.”

Léa, a regular since 2021
